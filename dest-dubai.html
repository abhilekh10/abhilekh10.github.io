Your 4-Night Journey

A typical itinerary — fully customizable to your preferences

Day 1: Arrival at Burj Al Arab

Private airport transfer to your Burj Al Arab suite. Meet your butler, explore the resort, and enjoy dinner at a Michelin-starred restaurant overlooking the Gulf at sunset.

Day 2: Desert Adventure

Morning camel trek through golden dunes. Visit a private desert camp with traditional Bedouin hospitality. Afternoon shopping at Gold Souk with expert guides. Evening stargazing with an astronomer.

Day 3: Urban Luxury & Beach

Personal shopping session at Dubai Mall with personal stylist. Exclusive boutique visits. Afternoon at a private beach club with cabana service. Evening yacht cruise with champagne and dinner.

Day 4: Departure

Leisurely breakfast overlooking the Gulf. Spa treatment or last-minute shopping. Private transfer to the airport for your onward journey.

Visa & Logistics

Indian Nationals: Visa-on-arrival available for Indian citizens. Simply present your passport at Dubai immigration. Processing takes minutes—no advance documentation needed.

Getting There: Fly to Dubai International Airport. We arrange all transfers, ground transportation, and private vehicle services throughout your stay.

Best Time to Visit: October–April (perfect weather, 20–30°C, low humidity). May–September is extremely hot (40°C+) but offers fewer crowds and lower prices. Year-round destination with excellent year-round amenities.
