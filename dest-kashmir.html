The Kashmir Experience

Alpine beauty and water-based luxury

🏠

Luxury Houseboat Stays

Stay in beautifully appointed wooden houseboats on Dal Lake with intricately carved interiors. Private decks for sunrise/sunset viewing and personalized service from your houseboat staff.

🚣

Shikara Adventures

Private shikara (traditional wooden boat) rides through the floating gardens. Morning mist tours, sunset cruises with refreshments, and visits to lotus gardens and local villages.

🏔️

Alpine Meadow Treks

Guided treks to stunning meadows—Gulmarg, Pahalgam, Sonmarg. Professional guides, picnic lunches at scenic vistas, and photography opportunities amid pristine mountain landscapes.

🍽️

Wazwan Culinary Experiences

Experience Kashmir's legendary Wazwan cuisine—22+ course meals prepared traditionally. Cooking classes with local chefs and dining experiences featuring regional spices and techniques.

🛒

Local Artisan Experiences

Visit carpet weaving workshops, papier-mâché studios, and wooden craft artisans. Meet craftspeople, learn traditional techniques, and commission bespoke pieces as souvenirs.

🧘

Wellness & Meditation

Yoga and meditation sessions overlooking the lake. Ayurvedic treatments using local herbs. Wellness consultations and rejuvenation programs tailored to your needs.

Your 5-Night Journey

A typical itinerary — fully customizable to your preferences

Day 1: Arrival on Dal Lake

Arrive in Srinagar and transfer to your luxury houseboat. Settle in, enjoy the lake views, and experience your first Wazwan dinner prepared on your boat.

Day 2: Shikara Exploration

Morning shikara ride through floating gardens with lotus viewing. Visit local villages and meet traditional boatmen. Afternoon meditation and spa treatment. Evening sunset cruise with refreshments.

Day 3: Alpine Meadow Trek

Full-day trek to Gulmarg meadows with picnic lunch at scenic vistas. Enjoy panoramic mountain views and photography opportunities. Return for dinner at your houseboat.

Day 4: Arts, Crafts & Culinary

Visit carpet weaving workshops and papier-mâché studios. Cooking class learning traditional Wazwan preparation. Evening shopping for local crafts and souvenirs.

Day 5: Departure

Morning yoga on your houseboat deck. Final shikara ride or meadow walk. Transfer to airport for your onward journey.

Visa & Logistics

Indian Nationals: No visa required for domestic travel. Simply present your ID proof for travel to Kashmir. Highly accessible for Indian citizens.

Getting There: Fly to Srinagar International Airport. We arrange private transfers to your houseboat. Scenic drive through Kashmir Valley.

Best Time to Visit: April–October (pleasant weather, 15–25°C, clear skies). May–September offers optimal hiking conditions. Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) are ideal. Winter is snowy and cold.
