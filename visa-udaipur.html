Eligibility & Requirements

Who Needs a Visa

  • Indian citizens do not need a visa to visit India
  • Valid Indian ID or passport sufficient
  • Domestic travel requires ID proof only
  • Can stay indefinitely with valid residency
  • Perfect for exploration and cultural tourism

Required Documents

  • Valid Indian passport or national ID
  • PAN card (for financial transactions)
  • Aadhar card (beneficial for some transactions)
  • Address proof if changing residence
  • Vaccination records (as per health guidelines)
  • Insurance documents (if applicable)

Visa Processing

  • No visa application process required
  • Internal travel document verification only
  • Standard domestic travel procedures
  • ID checks at airports and transportation
  • Minimal documentation required
  • Quick processing at check-in counters

Important Notes

  • Indian citizens have unrestricted access
  • Can stay as long as needed
  • Free movement throughout India
  • No entry stamps or visa requirements
  • Full economic and social access
  • Can conduct business and work without restrictions

Embassy & Information

  • Regional Tourism Information: Rajasthan Tourism Board
  • Local Contact: Udaipur Tourist Helpline
  • Website: www.rajassthan.gov.in
  • All government offices accessible to citizens

Travel Tips

  • Carry valid identity proof while traveling
  • Keep copies of important documents
  • Travel insurance recommended
  • Udaipur is easily accessible by flight, train, or road
  • Domestic flights are affordable and frequent
  • Hotel bookings essential during peak season
