Your 5-Night Journey

A typical itinerary — fully customizable to your preferences

Day 1: Arrival & Acclimatization in Leh

Arrive in Leh, check into luxury camp, rest and acclimatize to 11,500 ft altitude. Gentle walk in city, visit Shanti Stupa. Welcome dinner with local cuisine.

Day 2: Mountain Pass Expedition

Morning excursion to Khardung La (18,380 ft) with professional guides and support. Afternoon return and rest. Evening astronomy session with stargazing under pristine skies.

Day 3: Monastic Culture & Landscapes

Visit Hemis and Lamayuru monasteries with monk encounters. Afternoon photo expedition capturing otherworldly landscape. Evening meditation session at camp.

Day 4: Pangong Tso Lake & Stargazing

Full-day drive to Pangong Tso (14,270 ft), the world's highest saltwater lake. Pristine blue waters against desert mountains. Night under stars with professional astrophotography.

Day 5: Departure

Final morning trek or exploration. Cultural shopping for local crafts. Transfer to Leh for departure flights. Last-minute monastery visits if time permits.

Visa & Logistics

Indian Nationals: No visa required for domestic travel. Permits required for Pangong Tso area (we arrange). Restricted area permits obtained during planning phase.

Getting There: Fly to Leh Kushok Bakula Rimpochee Airport. We arrange private 4x4 transfers to camps. Scenic flights from Delhi available for added luxury.

Best Time to Visit: June–September (roads open, weather clear, 15–25°C daytime, -5 to 0°C nights). July–August is peak season with occasional rain. Early June and September offer fewer crowds. October routes may close due to snow.
