Eligibility & Requirements

Who Needs a Visa

  • Indian citizens require a Schengen visa for Italy
  • Visa valid for all Schengen countries
  • Tourist visas commonly issued for leisure travel
  • Multiple entry options available
  • Visa valid for up to 1 year depending on approval

Required Documents

  • Valid passport with 6+ months validity
  • Completed Schengen visa application form
  • 2 recent passport-sized photos (4.5 x 3.5 cm)
  • Hotel and accommodation bookings
  • Round-trip flight tickets
  • Bank statements for last 3 months
  • Employment letter or financial proof
  • Travel insurance (minimum 30,000 EUR)
  • Proof of residence in India

Visa Processing

  • Apply at Italian Embassy in New Delhi
  • Appointments required for application submission
  • Standard processing: 15-30 calendar days
  • Express processing: 5-7 business days (premium fee)
  • Interviews may be conducted
  • Biometric data collection may be required

Important Notes

  • Schengen visa covers Italy and 26 other countries
  • Tourist visa for leisure and tourism only
  • Cannot engage in paid employment
  • Visa valid for specified dates only
  • Early return does not extend visa validity
  • Cannot apply for extensions in Italy

Embassy & Information

  • Italian Embassy in New Delhi: 50-E, Chanakyapuri, New Delhi
  • Visa Centers: Multiple cities including Mumbai, Bangalore
  • Website: www.ambasciata.esteri.it
  • Online appointment system available

Travel Tips

  • Apply 6-8 weeks before intended travel
  • All documents must be clearly photocopied
  • Keep original documents as backups
  • Travel dates should align with hotel bookings
  • Bank statements must show consistent balance
  • Proof of ties to India strengthens application
