About us

We're a small team that plans trips
the way we'd want ours planned.

Nine years, a few thousand itineraries, and one stubborn rule: never sell someone a holiday we wouldn't take ourselves.

How we started

Bookbindass began in 2016 with a single Excel sheet of hotel contacts and a lot of late-night WhatsApp messages. Friends kept asking the same question — where should we actually go, and is this package worth it? — and the honest answer usually took a conversation, not a brochure.

So we built the business around the conversation. Every itinerary still starts with someone asking what you want out of the week, and ends with a plan you could hand to a stranger and have them understand exactly where they're sleeping, what it costs, and who to call.

What we believe

Advice beats inventory. Anyone can resell a hotel. Knowing that the north Goa beach you saw on Instagram will be unbearable in December, and the south one won't, is the part worth paying for — so we don't charge for it.

Prices should be itemised. Stay, transfers, activities, taxes, our margin. If you can't see what a line costs, you can't tell whether it's worth it.

Support doesn't end at departure. Flights move, weather turns, ferries get cancelled. Someone from the team answers the phone while you're away, including at 2am.

The events side

Somewhere along the way the trips turned into events. Clients who had us fly a family to Bali started asking whether we could also put on the party when they got home — and because we were already booking venues, transfers and hospitality, we could. Today we produce celebrity nights, private and launch parties, corporate events, weddings and spiritual programmes, handling the artist, the venue, the production and the guest logistics as one job.

We've hosted live shows with Parmish Verma and Sara Gurpal, and organised a spiritual event with Jaya Kishori. The useful part for you isn't the names — it's that the team has run rooms that size before, and knows what goes wrong in them.

Where we go

We plan across the Maldives, Mauritius, Bali, Thailand, Singapore and Dubai, and right across India — Kashmir and Himachal in the north, Sikkim and the Andamans in the east, Kerala and Goa in the south. If you want somewhere we don't list, ask anyway; we'll tell you honestly whether we're the right people for it.
