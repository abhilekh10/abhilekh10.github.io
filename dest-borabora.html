The Bora Bora Experience

Tropical island paradise in its purest form

🏡

Overwater Villas with Glass Floors

Wake to the lagoon beneath your bed. Glass-floor villas let you watch tropical fish glide past while sipping your morning coffee. Your private deck overlooks turquoise waters with Mt. Otemanu as backdrop.

🤿

Lagoon Snorkeling & Diving

Pristine reefs steps from your villa. Guided snorkeling with rays and sharks in their natural habitat. Certified diving expeditions to deeper trenches reveal vibrant coral gardens untouched by time.

🛥️

Private Boat Excursions

Explore neighboring islands by private speedboat or traditional outrigger. Visit Tupai atoll, picnic on pristine sandbars, and discover hidden coves known only to locals.

🍽️

Polynesian Fine Dining

Meals prepared with fresh catches and tropical ingredients. Beachfront dining on your toes, or chef-prepared cuisine in your villa overlooking the lagoon. Traditional feasts and modern fusion available.

🧘

Overwater Spa & Wellness

Treatments delivered to your villa or at the spa over water. Coconut oil massages, tropical flower baths, and yoga sessions with unobstructed ocean views create pure serenity.

🌅

Sunset Ceremonies & Romance

Vow renewals, marriage ceremonies, or romantic dinners orchestrated with perfection. We handle permits, flowers, champagne, and photography—just bring your loved one.

Your 5-Night Journey

A typical itinerary — fully customizable to your preferences

Day 1: Arrival in Paradise

Arrive via Tahiti (regional flights arranged). Speedboat transfer to your overwater villa. Settle in, swim in your private lagoon, and enjoy welcome cocktails at sunset.

Day 2: Lagoon Discovery

Morning snorkeling with rays and tropical fish. Afternoon boat excursion to Tupai atoll with lunch on a private sandbank. Evening relaxation massage in your villa.

Day 3: Water Activities & Dining

Full-day diving expedition to coral gardens with certified guides. Return for a Polynesian feast prepared beachfront with local dancers and musicians.

Day 4: Island Exploration

Private boat to neighboring islands and hidden coves. Snorkeling, beachcombing, and picnicking. Evening spa treatment in your overwater villa.

Day 5: Departure

Leisurely morning in your villa. Last-minute water activities or spa. Romantic lunch before your speedboat transfer back to Tahiti for onward flights.

Visa & Logistics

Indian Nationals: Visa-free entry to French Polynesia for 90 days. Your passport is your only requirement. Entry is straightforward with no advance paperwork needed.

Getting There: Fly to Papeete (Tahiti) via Paris or Asian hubs. Regional flights connect to Bora Bora in 50 minutes, or take our scenic speedboat (3 hours). We arrange all transfers.

Best Time to Visit: May–October (dry season, 24–28°C). November–April is warm but rainy and hurricane season (Nov-April). Year-round destination but peak months offer clearest waters.
