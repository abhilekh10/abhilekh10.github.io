The Seychelles Experience

Untouched island paradise in the Indian Ocean

Private Island Beach Resorts

Secluded beaches with granite boulders and emerald waters. Stay in beachfront villas with direct sand access. Your own private slice of paradise with staff attending to every whim.

World-Class Snorkeling & Diving

Pristine reefs teeming with tropical fish, manta rays, and sea turtles. Guided snorkeling expeditions and certified diving courses in crystal-clear waters.

Island Hopping Boat Cruises

Explore neighboring islands—Praslin, La Digue, Curieuse. Private boat excursions with gourmet picnics on pristine sandbars and unique beaches.

Creole Culinary Experiences

Meals featuring fresh seafood, tropical fruits, and Creole spices. Cooking classes with local chefs, beachfront dining, and private chef services in your villa.

Nature Trails & Wildlife

Guided hikes through lush forests. Encounter endemic bird species, giant tortoises, and pristine valleys. Naturalist guides share the island's ecological wonders.

Spa & Wellness Retreats

Island-inspired treatments using tropical oils and botanicals. Beachfront yoga, meditation sessions, and holistic wellness programs designed for rejuvenation.

Your 5-Night Journey

A typical itinerary — fully customizable to your preferences

Day 1: Arrival in Paradise

Arrive in Mahe and transfer to your private beach resort. Settle in, swim in crystal-clear waters, and enjoy welcome drinks at sunset.

Day 2: Snorkeling & Reef Exploration

Morning snorkeling expedition with marine naturalist. Afternoon beach relaxation and spa treatment. Evening Creole feast on the beach.

Day 3: Island Hopping Adventure

Full-day boat cruise to Praslin and La Digue with gourmet picnic. Explore unique beaches and pristine coves. Return for sunset cocktails.

Day 4: Nature & Culinary Experiences

Morning nature trail hike with wildlife spotting. Afternoon cooking class with local chef. Evening private dining in your villa.

Day 5: Departure

Leisurely beach morning. Spa treatment or final snorkeling. Transfer to airport for your onward flights.

Visa & Logistics

Indian Nationals: Visa-free entry for 30 days. Your passport is your only requirement. Upon arrival, a tourist visa stamp is issued at no charge.

Getting There: Fly to Seychelles International Airport on Mahe. We arrange private transfers to your resort island via boat.

Best Time to Visit: April–May and September–November (dry season, warm, 24–28°C). June–August offers cooler water and strong winds. Year-round destination with tropical charm.
