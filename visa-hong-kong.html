Eligibility & Requirements

Who Needs a Visa

  • Indian citizens get visa-free entry
  • No visa required for tourism
  • Up to 90 days stay allowed
  • Passport required for entry

Required Documents

  • Valid passport with 6+ months validity
  • Return flight ticket or onward travel
  • Hotel booking (recommended)
  • Proof of financial means
  • Travel insurance (optional)

Visa Processing

  • No visa required - entry on arrival
  • Simply show passport at immigration
  • Get stamp for 90 days stay
  • Instant approval at airport
  • Very convenient for Indian citizens

Important Notes

  • Hong Kong grants visa-free entry
  • 90 days stay allowed on arrival
  • Very business-friendly destination
  • English widely spoken (ex-British colony)
  • Cannot work on tourist entry
  • Visa-free benefits apply for tourism

Embassy & Consulates

  • No visa required for entry
  • Hong Kong Tourism Board: www.discoverhongkong.com
  • Immigration Department: +852-2824-6111
  • Airport immigration available 24/7

Travel Tips

  • No visa application needed - huge advantage
  • Victoria Peak - best city views
  • HKD is currency (linked to USD)
  • Excellent transportation network
  • Year-round tourism destination
  • September-November: best weather
