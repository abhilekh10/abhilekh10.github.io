The Kyoto Experience

Ancient traditions meet contemporary luxury

🏨

Premium Ryokan Stays

Stay in an exclusive ryokan with private onsen (hot spring baths), kaiseki dinners, and tatami rooms overlooking gardens. Personal attendants cater to your every need with traditional Japanese hospitality.

🍵

Private Tea Ceremonies

Learn the art of tea in temple gardens with master practitioners. Experience the Zen philosophy through this meditative ritual, followed by traditional sweets in serene surroundings.

⛩️

Temple & Shrine Explorations

Private guided tours of Kyoto's most iconic temples—Kinkaku-ji, Fushimi Inari, Kiyomizu-dera. Early morning visits before crowds, with expert historian guides sharing the spiritual significance.

🎎

Geisha & Cultural Encounters

Meet geishas in the historic Gion district. Attend traditional dance performances or private dining experiences with entertaining artists who have trained for decades.

🎨

Traditional Arts & Crafts

Learn calligraphy, flower arranging (ikebana), or pottery from master artisans. Visits to silk weaving studios and workshops where 400-year-old techniques are still practiced.

🍜

Kaiseki Culinary Mastery

Multi-course kaiseki dinners prepared with seasonal ingredients and served with seasonal flowers. Private chef experiences and cooking classes teaching the art of Japanese cuisine.

Your 5-Night Journey

A typical itinerary — fully customizable to your preferences

Day 1: Arrival in Kyoto

Arrive and transfer to your luxury ryokan. Settle in, enjoy your private onsen, and experience your first kaiseki dinner with the ryokan's master chef.

Day 2: Tea Ceremony & Temples

Morning private tea ceremony in a temple garden with a master practitioner. Afternoon guided tour of Kinkaku-ji and Kiyomizu-dera temples. Evening geisha performance in Gion.

Day 3: Arts, Crafts & Culture

Calligraphy lesson with a master artist. Afternoon flower arranging (ikebana) class. Visit a silk weaving studio. Evening kaiseki dinner at your ryokan.

Day 4: Early Morning Temple Walks & Artisan Encounters

Early visit to Fushimi Inari's 10,000 red torii gates before crowds. Pottery workshop with a local artisan. Afternoon free for shopping or relaxation. Evening traditional dance performance.

Day 5: Departure

Final onsen bath and leisurely breakfast. Last-minute temple visits or shopping for traditional crafts. Private transfer to airport.

Visa & Logistics

Indian Nationals: Visa required for Japan. We assist with visa applications and can recommend trusted agencies. Processing takes 2-3 weeks. Tourist visas allow stays up to 90 days.

Getting There: Fly to Kansai International Airport (Osaka), then scenic train to Kyoto (75 minutes). We arrange all transfers and rail bookings.

Best Time to Visit: March–May (cherry blossoms, 15–20°C) and September–November (clear skies, fall colors, 15–25°C). Summer is humid; winter is quiet and cold. Avoid golden week (late April) for fewer crowds.
