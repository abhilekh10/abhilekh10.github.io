Eligibility & Requirements

Who Needs a Visa

  • Indian citizens can enter French Polynesia visa-free
  • Visa-free stay valid for tourism purposes
  • Valid for 90 days from entry date
  • No prior authorization needed
  • Entry stamp provided on arrival

Required Documents

  • Valid passport with 6+ months validity
  • Return flight ticket
  • Hotel reservation or accommodation proof
  • Proof of financial means
  • Travel insurance (recommended)
  • Completed arrival card (provided on flight)
  • Valid travel documents for onward journey

Visa Processing

  • Visa-free entry for Indian nationals
  • No pre-arrival visa application required
  • Immigration processed at Faa International Airport
  • Entry stamp issued on arrival (under 5 minutes)
  • No visa fees applicable
  • Departure procedures standard

Important Notes

  • Visa-free stay permitted for tourism and leisure
  • Cannot engage in paid work or business
  • Stay cannot be extended beyond 90 days
  • Must maintain valid return flight
  • French Polynesian authorities may request proof of funds
  • Overstay not permitted

Embassy & Information

  • French Embassy in New Delhi: 2/50-E, Shantipath, Chanakyapuri
  • Information: French Polynesia tourism website
  • Currency: CPF Franc (CFP)
  • No separate visa application needed

Travel Tips

  • Book accommodations in advance
  • Confirm return flight 72 hours before departure
  • Keep copy of hotel booking with immigration
  • Arrival card must be completed accurately
  • Keep passport stamp clear for exit
  • French Polynesia has limited ATM facilities - carry sufficient funds
