Your 6-Night Journey

A typical itinerary — fully customizable to your preferences

Day 1: Arrival in Zermatt

Private transfer from Geneva or Zurich airport. Settle into your luxury mountain chalet with mountain views and private spa. Evening welcome dinner at your chalet with local wines.

Day 2: Helicopter Skiing Adventure

Early departure for helicopter skiing on untouched slopes. Professional guide and photographer accompany you. Return for a relaxing spa treatment and gourmet lunch.

Day 3: Mountaineering & Glacier Walk

Guided ascent of a scenic peak with professional climbers. Glacier walks with stunning panoramic views. Evening fondue dinner in your chalet with premium Swiss wines.

Day 4: Wine Tasting & Regional Exploration

Visit renowned Alpine wineries and cheese makers. Private tasting sessions with producers. Return to chalet for Michelin-inspired raclette dinner prepared by your private chef.

Day 5: Spa Day & Scenic Walks

Morning yoga overlooking the peaks. Full-day spa treatments at your private facilities. Leisurely walks through Alpine meadows. Evening at your own pace with fine dining available.

Day 6: Departure

Leisurely breakfast with mountain views. Last-minute shopping or relaxation. Private transfer back to Geneva or Zurich for your onward flights.

Visa & Logistics

Indian Nationals: Schengen visa required. We assist with visa applications and can recommend trusted agencies. Processing takes 2-3 weeks. Most tourist activities do not require additional permits.

Getting There: Fly to Geneva or Zurich, then enjoy scenic train or private transfers to mountain destinations. We arrange all ground transportation.

Best Time to Visit: December–March for skiing and winter activities (snow conditions optimal). Summer (June–August) offers hiking, mountaineering, and pleasant weather at 15–20°C. September–October brings clear skies and fewer crowds.
