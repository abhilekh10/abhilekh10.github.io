Your 6-Night Journey

A typical itinerary — fully customizable to your preferences

Day 1: Arrival in Positano

Arrive at Naples airport. Private transfer along the Amalfi Drive to your clifftop villa. Settle in, explore your private terrace, and dinner at a family-run trattoria.

Day 2: Yacht Exploration

Full-day private yacht excursion. Visit Li Galli islands, swim in hidden grottoes, lunch on the boat with caught fish, explore Capri if desired. Return for sunset aperitivo.

Day 3: Ravello Discovery

Private car and driver guide you to Ravello's clifftop gardens. Visit Villa d'Este and Villa Rufolo. Afternoon shopping for artisan ceramics and limoncello. Michelin dinner with views.

Day 4: Beach Club & Water Time

Relaxed beach day at Positano's private clubs. Kayaking, paddle boarding, or simply swimming in your private cove. Spa treatment in your villa or beachfront.

Day 5: Lemon Groves & Hidden Villages

Visit family-owned lemon farms producing the famous Limoncello. Explore the tiny villages of Furore and Praiano. Cooking class with local chef or private pottery lesson.

Day 6: Departure

Final morning swim or explore local markets. Leisurely lunch at your villa. Private transfer back to Naples for your onward flights.

Visa & Logistics

Indian Nationals: EU citizens require a Schengen visa. We provide detailed guidance and recommend trusted visa agencies.

Getting There: Fly into Naples airport. We arrange all transfers. Driving the Amalfi Coast road is scenic but challenging—we provide experienced drivers throughout.

Best Time to Visit: April–May and September–October (warm, manageable crowds). June–August is peak season with higher prices. November–March is quieter with occasional closures in smaller establishments.
