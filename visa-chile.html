Eligibility & Requirements

Who Needs a Visa

  • Indian citizens require visa for Chile
  • Tourist visa for leisure travel
  • Long and narrow South American country
  • Popular for adventure tourism

Required Documents

  • Valid passport with 6+ months validity
  • Completed visa application form
  • Passport-sized color photos (2)
  • Hotel booking confirmation
  • Round-trip flight tickets
  • Bank statements for financial proof
  • Employment letter
  • Travel insurance (recommended)

Visa Processing

  • Apply at Chilean Embassy or Consulate
  • In-person submission required
  • Processing: 5-10 working days
  • Visa fees payable
  • May require interview

Important Notes

  • Chile most developed in South America
  • Atacama Desert - world driest
  • Tourist visa 90 days
  • Cannot work on tourist visa
  • Patagonia region breathtaking
  • Very safe for tourism

Embassy & Consulates

  • Chilean Embassy, New Delhi: Chanakya Puri
  • Website: https://www.explora.com/chile
  • Visa Services: +91-11-4163-4600
  • Contact for visa details

Travel Tips

  • Apply 2-3 weeks in advance
  • Atacama Desert must-see
  • Patagonia for trekking
  • Chilean Peso (CLP) currency
  • December-February: summer
  • Very organized country
